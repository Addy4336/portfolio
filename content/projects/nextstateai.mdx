## What it is

Two apps in one Turborepo: a Next.js CMS where the team manages listings, owners and
operations, and a Vite + React frontend where buyers browse. Supabase holds the data,
Better Auth handles sign-in, and the CMS syncs with Zoho Creator and Google Drive.
